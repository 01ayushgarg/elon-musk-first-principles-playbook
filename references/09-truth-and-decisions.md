## Foundations first

> "If you cannot count on the foundational physics being correct, obviously the inventions are simply wishful
> thinking, imagination land. Magic basically." [LEX400 00:37:02]

> "Physics is just deepening what's inside into how reality works. And then there's engineering which is
> inventing things that have never existed. Now the range of possibilities for engineering is far greater than
> for physics." [LEX400 00:38:38]
