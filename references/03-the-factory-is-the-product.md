## Manufacturing realities

> "This is the essential quandary of manufacturing: factories need continuous production for efficiency, but
> consumer demand is seasonal." [X 2024-02-11](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1756751879701156059)

Simplify the menu to simplify the line: "Moving 2 of 7 Tesla colors off menu on Wednesday to simplify
manufacturing." [X 2018-09-11](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1039390759907020801)

> "Manufacturing is awesome!" [X 2022-10-22](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1583909603250622464)

**Applying it beyond hardware** (our reading, not his words): in software or services, "the factory" is the
system that produces the product every day: your onboarding, delivery, support or content pipeline. Treat
that system as a product with its own design, metrics and iteration.

**Checks to run:**
1. What's your "factory"? Who owns its design?
2. How much effort goes into the thing vs the system that produces it?
3. When did you last do the front-line work yourself?
4. Which options or variants could you cut to simplify production?
