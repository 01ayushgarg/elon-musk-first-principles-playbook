# 00 · Who Elon Musk is, for this playbook

This playbook is about one thing: how Elon Musk says he thinks about engineering, manufacturing, cost, speed and
building companies. It doesn't cover his politics, his personal life or his social media disputes.

What the sources here cover:
- **SpaceX:** reusable rockets, Starship, and the Starbase factory he walked through on camera in 2021 [SB1, SB2,
  SB3].
- **Tesla:** the 2006 and 2016 master plans he signed [MP1, MP2], and three years he says he spent living in the
  Fremont and Nevada factories [TED22].
- **Neuralink and xAI:** his Lex Fridman interviews in 2019, 2023 and 2024 [LEX49, LEX400, LEX438].

On why he started Tesla: "the overarching purpose of Tesla Motors (and the reason I am funding the company) is to
help expedite the move from a mine-and-burn hydrocarbon economy towards a solar electric economy" [MP1, 2006].

On SpaceX's mission: "So for SpaceX, the goal is to establish a self-sustaining city on Mars." [LEX438 01:22:19]

## Read him with his own caveats

> "I would say that I'm pathologically optimistic on schedule. This is true." [LEX400 01:54:38]

> "And now I have personally made the mistake of going backwards on all five steps multiple times." [SB1 22:02]

Take the methods. Treat his timelines as aspirations.
