Rules:
1. **One line.** If you list three, you haven't found it.
2. **Name a person**, not a team.
3. **Go there.** If it's the onboarding flow, sit with users onboarding. If it's sales, take the calls.
4. **Drill down** to a part or a person: *power* becomes *turbines* becomes *blades* [DW 00:00:00].
5. **Predict the next one.** When this limit goes, what will stop you next? Start on it before it bites.
