## Labelling rules

- **His words** are in quotation marks with a citation. Nothing else is.
- **Our reading** (interpretations, translations to software) and **our suggestion** (steps, thresholds,
  templates, decision rules) are marked as ours.
- **`[sic]`** marks a caption mishearing in the Starbase videos, with the likely word where it's clear (for
  example "usable [sic: reusable]"). It is used only for the Starbase caption tracks. Official transcripts are
  quoted as published.
- **Trims** inside a quote are marked "...". No quote is longer than about 60 words.
- **Fictional examples** are labelled as fictional, and every number in them is invented.

## Conflicts and caveats

- **Schedules.** His timelines are often late, and he says so ("pathologically optimistic on schedule"
  [LEX400 01:54:38]). In 2026 he explained that he sets deadlines at about a 50% chance [DW 01:44:16]. The
  playbook uses his methods, not his dates.
- **The order of the algorithm.** He gives the same five steps in the same order in 2021 [SB1] and 2024
  [LEX438].
- **Starship material.** The 2016 design was carbon fiber [NS17, IAC17]; the 2026 interview explains the later
  switch to stainless steel [DW]. Both are cited where relevant, with dates.
- **Retellings.** The Dwarkesh interview is his account, years later, of decisions such as the steel switch and
  his hiring habits. It's his view, not an independent record.
- **Abridged and edited.** IAC17 is abridged by SpaceX; NS17 is an edited version of a talk.
- **Master Plan Part 3 (2023) and Part IV (2025)** are signed by The Tesla Team, not by Musk. They are not
  quoted as his words.
- **Starlink and Starship.** These sources don't contain him saying that Starlink funds Starship. Chapter 07
  says so.

## What we didn't use, and why

- **Idiot index**: widely attributed to him, mostly through his biography. It doesn't appear in the
  first-party sources above, so it isn't quoted. Chapter 04 describes the idea in our own words.
- **Biographies and profiles** (including Walter Isaacson's 2023 biography): secondary sources. Not used.
- **Lex Fridman #18 and #252**: no official transcript to check quotes against.
- **The Hyperloop Alpha technical section**: co-written, so not quoted as his words.
- **Third-party quote compilations, "Musk's rules" listicles and paraphrase sites**: not used.
- **Anything about politics, government, his personal life or disputes**: out of scope, including whole
  chapters of the 2026 interview.

## How quotes were checked

Before publishing, we saved a plain-text copy of every source (the official transcript, the archived blog post
or PDF, the post text from X, and for Starbase the YouTube caption tracks: excerpts around the quoted passages
for Parts 1 and 2, the full track for Part 3). A script then checked that every quote is an exact substring of
the saved copy of the source it cites, ignoring only quote-mark style, whitespace, `[sic]` notes and "..." trims.
A second check confirmed no quote is over 60 words.

What this does **not** prove: the Starbase captions are machine-made, so a Starbase quote matches the captions,
not necessarily what he said; timestamps there are approximate. The saved copies are not redistributed in this
repo, so the check can't be re-run from here; every source has a link above so you can check any quote by
hand.
