## Labelling rules

- **His words** are in quotation marks with a citation. Nothing else is.
- **Our reading** (translations to software, checks, templates) is marked as ours.
- **`[sic]`** marks a caption mishearing in the Starbase videos, with the likely word where it's clear (for
  example "usable [sic: reusable]"). The Starbase quotes come from YouTube caption tracks, not an official
  transcript.
- **Fictional examples** are labelled as fictional.

## Conflicts and caveats

- **Schedules.** His timelines are often late, and he says so ("pathologically optimistic on schedule"
  [LEX400 01:54:38]). The playbook uses his methods, not his dates.
- **The order of the algorithm.** He gives the same five steps in the same order in 2021 [SB1] and 2024
  [LEX438]. Where wording differs, both are quoted.
- **Master Plan Part 3 (2023) and Part IV (2025)** are signed by "The Tesla Team", not by Musk. They are not
  quoted as his words.

## What we didn't use, and why

- **"Idiot index"**: widely attributed to him, mostly through his biography. It doesn't appear in the
  first-party transcripts above, so it isn't quoted. Chapter 04 describes the idea in our own words.
- **Biographies and profiles** (including Walter Isaacson's 2023 biography): secondary sources. Not used.
- **Lex Fridman #18 and #252**: no official transcript to check quotes against.
- **Third-party quote compilations, "Musk's rules" listicles and paraphrase sites**: not used.
- **Anything about politics, his personal life or disputes**: out of scope.
